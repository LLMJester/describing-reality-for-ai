The five parts

Stage 2 takes the stage 1 winner apart into five parts. Each part has two versions: the winner's own ("-") and one new version ("+") written by Opus from a short summary of the winner's stage 1 results on the generated items (never the questions, the hand-written items or the scores by problem family). All 32 mixes are tested, so we learn what each part does on its own.

PartWhat it isThe new version ("+")
A notes layoutHow the notes are organised: their sections, keys or columns.A different organisation of the same content.
B fact writingThe rules for writing each fact down.A different set of writing rules.
C answeringHow Luna answers a question from its notes.A step-by-step answering procedure.
D worked exampleWhether the recipe shows an example of good notes.One short example (or none, if the winner had one).
E thinking budgetHow many tool calls (saves and reads of its notes) Luna may make while writing its notes; the limit is shown to Luna.Double the winner's budget (half, if double passes the maximum). Plain-prose notes also get one revision pass.

Interaction terms (synergy / antagonism)

How much two parts' effects depend on each other. A positive term can mean two harmful parts hurt less together, not that the pair helps: so each row also shows the design with just those two new parts, its score and its difference from design 1 (the winner unchanged).
